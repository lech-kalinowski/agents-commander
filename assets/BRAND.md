# Agents Commander — retro identity

Updated 2026-09-02. Original pixel-terminal concept inspired by the visual
language of Atari/Commodore-era home-computer software, not either company's
trademark. Two opposing prompts exchange one square command/cursor.

- [Square mark](logo.png): README, landing-page navigation/favicon/hero and
  conference deck.
- [Wordmark lockup](logo-wordmark.png): wider promotional applications.
- `landing-page/logo.png` and `landing-page/logo-wordmark.png` are exact copies
  for the standalone GitHub Pages directory.

The final PNGs intentionally use an opaque warm off-white background. Do not
treat them as transparent assets. Navy shapes and periwinkle accent contrast
with the off-white; preserve aspect ratio and clear space. These are generated
raster masters, not hand-hinted bitmap fonts or editable vectors. The terminal's
existing ASCII title is retained. The old mascot remains recoverable in git
history; no claim of trademark clearance is made.

## Generation provenance

Created with the built-in image-generation tool; no CLI/API fallback. The first
concept had framed terminals; refinement reduced it to opposing pixel prompts.
The refinement returned a baked-in checkerboard, so final background replacement
was requested explicitly. Only the two approved final assets are shipped.

Creative direction: minimalist 8-bit terminal developer-tool identity, precise
pixel-stepped forms, balanced negative space, exact AGENTS / COMMANDER spelling,
no Fuji mark, C= mark, mascot, gradient, glow, bevel or other brand's emblem.

Final lockup prompt (applied to the refined concept):

> Use case: background-extraction / logo-brand. This is the edit target. Change
> ONLY the background: completely remove the gray checkerboard pattern and
> replace it with one perfectly uniform solid warm off-white color #F6F4EB.
> This request deliberately wants an OPAQUE background, not transparency and
> not a checkerboard. Preserve the two opposing pixel chevrons, periwinkle
> center square, navy AGENTS / COMMANDER wordmark, exact lettering and existing
> proportions. Every element must be flat, crisp, no texture/gradient/shadow.
> Keep one horizontal logo lockup. Frame it with a compact even outer margin,
> about 5% of image width, rather than large unused vertical padding. Wide 3:1
> composition. No added objects or text.

Final icon prompt (applied to the same refined concept):

> Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Extract the emblem from the LEFT
> of this reference as a standalone square application icon. Preserve the exact
> design: two dark navy pixel-stepped chevrons facing a small periwinkle square
> (> square <). OMIT the entire AGENTS COMMANDER wordmark. Center just this symbol
> on a perfectly uniform solid warm off-white #F6F4EB opaque square background,
> with balanced generous 15% margin. No checkerboard, transparency, border,
> terminal window boxes, letterforms, bevel, gradient, shadow, texture or
> additional symbols. Crisp flat geometric pixel-grid edges, minimal
> Atari/Commodore-era original computer-software identity, no copies of either
> brand trademark.

The prompts record intent; actual image geometry/padding is defined by the
checked-in PNGs. Always visually verify the image at its final display size.
